<div class="sec-hdr"><h2>Okta Platform Architecture Overview</h2><p>The Identity Security Fabric — unified control plane for human, machine, and AI identities</p></div>
<div class="prose">Okta operates as a <strong>"Sovereign Identity Plane"</strong> — a vendor-neutral cloud identity platform that sits between your authoritative sources (HR, AD, LDAP) and your downstream applications (8,200+ in the OIN). It is architected as a layered fabric: <strong>Universal Directory</strong> masters identity data, the <strong>access policy engine</strong> evaluates every authentication request against risk signals, <strong>lifecycle management</strong> automates JML provisioning, and <strong>governance + privileged access</strong> enforce least privilege. Since 2025, this fabric now extends to non-human identities (service accounts, API keys, AI agents) with the same visibility and governance as human users.</div>
